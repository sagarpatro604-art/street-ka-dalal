Street ka Dalal: read the market like an institution

Free stock market research for Indian investors. Screeners, quarterly results and what the price did after them, sector rotation, mutual fund flows, pre-market and Dalal, an AI buddy. Built on NSE data, explained in plain words, free with Google sign-in.

Inside the terminal

  • Stock watch: your lists on one screen with our own chart and results markers.
  • Screeners: 27 end-of-day scans on every NSE stock.
  • Groups and rotation: sectors, industries and themes with trend labels and the RRG.
  • Results and Results Plus: every NSE result, gaps that held or filled, sectors after results, the profit pool and guidance changes.
  • Pre-market: GIFT Nifty, global cues, flows and the night's filings.
  • Mutual funds: where funds are buying and selling, stock by stock.
  • Discover, Portfolio X-ray, news and filings, the Library and Insights.
