How we check: we take up to 6 sentences from your text and search the web for them word for word. Then we open every page found and count, word by word, which runs of 7 or more words of your text appear on that page. The percentage is the share of your words inside such runs. We check the open web only (not paid journals, private papers or other students' work), and pages that block us cannot be checked, so treat a low number as "nothing found", not a guarantee.
